Our story

From a coffee cart to the corner of Tern Street

Little Tern started as a coffee cart on the foreshore. Locals kept asking for food, so we found a corner shop with big windows and a bench out front.

Our bread comes from a bakery in Port Melbourne, our beans from a Brunswick roaster, and our eggs from free-range farms in Gippsland.
